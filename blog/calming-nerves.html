How to Calm Your Nerves Before the Theory Test

Taking your theory test soon? Here are some practical ways to reduce anxiety and perform your best:

  • Practice deeply: Familiarity reduces fear. Go through flashcards until you feel confident.
  • Sleep well: Being well-rested improves focus and reduces stress.
  • Visualize success: Spend a few minutes imagining yourself confidently passing.
  • Arrive early: Avoid last-minute panic by getting there with time to spare.
  • Practice breathing: Deep breathing helps calm the nervous system quickly.
